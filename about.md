I'm Rohan Reddy. I work on systems around language models and agents, and I like understanding how things work well enough to explain them plainly. A lot of my day-to-day thinking comes back to a simple question: how do we know when an output is actually right?

This blog is where I work through that question in public. I write about inference, agentic systems, verification, and the small engineering choices that determine whether a promising idea survives contact with real use. I try to keep the essays grounded in papers, working systems, and things I would actually want to build.

Outside of that, I spend time with trading card games, especially *Magic: The Gathering* and *Riftbound*. I like the mix of rules, strategy, weird interactions, and the stories that emerge from a well-built deck. I also play video games, with *League of Legends* being the one I return to most often.

I read and write because both are good ways to pay attention. Some of my favorite authors are Gene Wolfe, especially *The Shadow of the Torturer*, Ursula K. Le Guin, and Kazuo Ishiguro. I like books that build strange worlds, take ideas seriously, and leave enough unsaid for the reader to keep turning them over.
