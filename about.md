I'm Rohan Reddy. I build systems that run models, and most of my attention goes to one question: how do you know the output is right?

This blog is where I think through that question in public. Some essays are about inference engineering: speculative decoding, KV caches, disaggregated serving, low-precision formats. Some are about agentic engineering: how to brief a worker model, when multi-agent designs pay off, what benchmarks actually measure. Running through all of them is verification: oracles, judges, rubrics, reward hacking, and the habit of checking a claim before building on it.

Every essay links its sources, and the numbers in the charts come from the cited papers.

Code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/roro2006).
